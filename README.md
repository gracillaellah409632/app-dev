My Favorite Series
The Twilight Saga
The Twilight Saga is one of my favorite film series based on the novels written by Stephenie Meyer. It is a fantasy romance story that follows the life of Bella Swan and her relationship with Edward Cullen, a vampire.

Main Characters
Bella Swan
Edward Cullen
Jacob Black
Alice Cullen
Carlisle Cullen
Movies in the Series
Twilight (2008)
The Twilight Saga: New Moon (2009)
The Twilight Saga: Eclipse (2010)
The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 1 (2011)
The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2 (2012)
Why I Like It
Interesting romance and fantasy storyline
Memorable characters
Exciting conflicts between vampires and werewolves
Beautiful soundtrack and cinematography
Strong themes of love, friendship, and family
